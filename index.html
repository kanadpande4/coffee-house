Our Story & Craft

Crafted With Passion, Served With Warmth

Founded with a simple belief that great coffee brings people together, Coffee House sources shade-grown Arabica beans directly from high-altitude estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg.

Every morning at 5:30 AM, our bakers laminate French butter croissants by hand and prepare sourdough loaves so your breakfast plate is as memorable as your first sip of espresso.
